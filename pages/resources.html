Official Agencies & Websites

Bookmark these sites for reliable, up-to-date information during typhoon season.

PAGASA (DOST)

Primary agency for official storm tracking, signal warnings, and flood advisories.

Main Web Portal: pagasa.dost.gov.ph Tropical Cyclone Bulletins: severe-weather-bulletin Official Facebook Page: PAGASA.DOST.GOV.PH

NDRRMC / OCD

Central command for national emergency coordination, severe weather advisories, and rescue deployment.

Main NDRRMC Portal: ndrrmc.gov.ph Office of Civil Defense (OCD): ocd.gov.ph Official Facebook Page: NDRRMC

DSWD DROMIC

Monitors live evacuation updates, relief goods distribution, and casualty/displaced population reports.

DROMIC Disaster Portal: dromic.dswd.gov.ph DSWD Main Portal: dswd.gov.ph Official Facebook Page: dswdserves

Philippine Coast Guard (PCG)

Manages flood rescues, maritime safety warnings, and sea travel suspensions during severe weather.

Main Web Portal: coastguard.gov.ph National Facebook Page: officialcoastguardph Coast Guard District Bicol Page: CGDBCL

Local Government & Community

Your barangay and LGU are your first line of defense. Know your local officials and evacuation centers before a storm hits.

Camarines Norte Provincial Government

The provincial LGU issues local calamity declarations, coordinates evacuation, and distributes relief. Follow their page for real-time updates during typhoons.

Your Barangay Hall

Know your barangay captain and tanod hotline. They issue the first evacuation orders and know which streets flood first. Register your family with the barangay emergency contact list.
