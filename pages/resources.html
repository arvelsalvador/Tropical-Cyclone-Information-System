Official Agencies & Websites

Bookmark these sites for reliable, up-to-date information during typhoon season.

PAGASA

The Philippine weather bureau. Check here for tropical cyclone bulletins, signal warnings, rainfall advisories, and forecast tracks updated every 6 hours.

pagasa.dost.gov.ph Tropical Cyclone Bulletins Warning Signals & Advisories

NDRRMC

The National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council coordinates government response during calamities. Check for official evacuation orders and relief operations.

ndrrmc.gov.ph Current Calamity Status

PHIVOLCS

The Philippine Institute of Volcanology and Seismology monitors volcanic activity and earthquakes — both of which can be triggered or worsened by typhoons.

phivolcs.dost.gov.ph

DSWD

The Department of Social Welfare and Development provides relief goods, family packs, and financial assistance to families affected by typhoons and other disasters.

dswd.gov.ph

Local Government & Community

Your barangay and LGU are your first line of defense. Know your local officials and evacuation centers before a storm hits.

Camarines Norte Provincial Government

The provincial LGU issues local calamity declarations, coordinates evacuation, and distributes relief. Follow their page for real-time updates during typhoons.

camarinesnorte.gov.ph

Your Barangay Hall

Know your barangay captain and tanod hotline. They issue the first evacuation orders and know which streets flood first. Register your family with the barangay emergency contact list.

Visit or call your barangay hall for the local emergency hotline
