i Did You Know?

The Philippines experiences an average of 20 typhoons every year.

Being informed and prepared can greatly reduce risks and save lives.

About 6 to 9 typhoons make landfall in the Philippines in an average year.

PAGASA raises 5 warning signal levels — Signal No. 5 is the highest and most dangerous.

Knowledge is our strongest defense.

Keep learning, stay informed, and help build a safer Camarines Norte.
